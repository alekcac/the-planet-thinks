As of 2026, Wikipedia holds more than 65 million articles across roughly 340 language editions, about 300 of which are actively edited. The numbers, question by question

How many Wikipedia articles are there?

Across all of its language editions, Wikipedia has more than 65 million articles as of 2026. They are spread over roughly 340 editions, of which about 300 see active editing. The count grows by thousands of new articles every day.

How many articles does English Wikipedia have?

English Wikipedia has more than seven million articles, making it the largest edition — yet it accounts for only around a tenth of Wikipedia's total article count across all languages.

How many Wikipedia pages are there?

Far more than articles. Counting talk pages, redirects, categories and project pages, English Wikipedia alone has over 60 million pages. The word "articles" refers only to actual encyclopedia entries in the main namespace.

How many language editions does Wikipedia have?

Roughly 340 language editions exist, and about 300 of them are actively edited. At any given minute, edits are typically arriving in dozens of languages at once — the mix follows daylight around the planet, as the live table above shows.

How many editors does Wikipedia have?

Roughly a quarter of a million people make at least one edit in a typical month across all editions, and a dedicated core of tens of thousands make more than 100 edits a month. The most prolific human editor has made over five million edits.

How many new articles are created per day?

On the order of 15,000 new articles are created across all language editions on a typical day. English Wikipedia alone adds roughly 500–600 new articles a day after quality checks.

How many gigabytes is Wikipedia?

Surprisingly few for the text: the compressed text of English Wikipedia's current articles is only about 25 GB — small enough to fit on a laptop. With full edit history and the media library on Wikimedia Commons, the complete project runs to hundreds of terabytes.
